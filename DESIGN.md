# The witness register
The title occupies three lines of Gloock, with the final word printed in mint on dark green. The rotating engraving of the critical strip fills the hero edge to edge. Its lines fade behind the title and claim button, with the clearest detail on the right. Ivory paper marks the transition from announcement to signature.

The badge takes the form of an engraved certificate with a numbered inscription panel. The participant name is the largest personal detail. The edition date and actual signing time have separate positions. A specimen is explicitly unnumbered. Registry rows are ordered by signature number and keep their numbers after deletions.
